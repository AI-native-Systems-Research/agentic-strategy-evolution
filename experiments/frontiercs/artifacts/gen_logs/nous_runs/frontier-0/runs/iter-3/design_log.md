## Summary

**Iteration 3** tests whether **integrated bitmap-based gap-filling** improves the polyomino packing score.

### Arms
- **h-main**: Enhanced skyline packer (v8) with a 2D bitmap maintained during placement. After the scoring loop selects the best candidate, the bitmap is scanned for gaps below the skyline — if the piece fits in a gap, it's placed there instead of on top, preventing unnecessary height increases.
- **h-control-negative**: Unmodified iter-2 skyline packer (v7) — establishes the baseline without gap-filling.

### Key findings from exploration
- The gap-filling mechanism is sound: it fills internal gaps that pure skyline tracking masks. One clean back-to-back test showed +1.3 points.
- However, score variance from system load (~±5 points) dwarfs the algorithmic improvement (~0-2 points), making single-run comparisons unreliable.
- Gravity compaction, extra orderings, and combined approaches were all tried and EXCLUDED because they added overhead without reliable improvement.
- The reference solution (IIMOC human best) and our v7 achieve essentially identical scores (~82-86 depending on load).
